This project studies how diffusion language models and autoregressive language models behave under controlled behavioral perturbations drawn from CheckList-style evaluation suites.

The evaluation covers sentiment, QQP, and SQuAD-style tasks across eight instruction-tuned models, with analysis of pass-rate gaps, perturbation coefficients, capability-level behavior, selective stability, and label-prior effects.

**Publication:** *Grounding Language Models: Learning Faithfully and Efficiently @ EMNLP 2026*  
**Authors:** Aarush Rathore, Tirtharaj Dash, Lovekesh Vig, Ashwin Srinivasan
