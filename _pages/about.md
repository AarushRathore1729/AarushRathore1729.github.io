I’m **Aarush Rathore**, a final-year Electronics and Communication Engineering undergraduate at **BITS Pilani, Goa Campus**. I’m interested in building and studying machine-learning systems: efficient training and inference, language models, retrieval and agentic systems, and the systems work that makes modern AI reliable and usable.

My recent research studies the robustness of **diffusion and autoregressive language models** under CheckList-style behavioral perturbations. The work appeared at the *Grounding Language Models: Learning Faithfully and Efficiently* workshop at **EMNLP 2026**.

On the engineering side, I like projects that sit between models and systems. Recent work includes **SpecVoice / Fast Whisper**, which explores speculative decoding for low-latency Whisper inference, and a healthcare-oriented **RAG pipeline** developed during my internship work at Docquity.

### Current interests

- ML systems and efficient inference
- Language models, diffusion models, and generative modeling
- Backend and distributed systems for AI applications
- Retrieval, agents, evaluation, and reliability
- Robotics and embodied AI

You can find code and ongoing work on [GitHub](https://github.com/AarushRathore1729).
